# Changelog

All notable changes to Firefly Focus are documented here.
This project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [2.5.0] — 2026-08-10

First Chrome Web Store release.

### Added
- **Runs as an installable app, not just an extension.** The same code now also serves a standalone PWA: `index.html` is the timer in its own window, `ambient.html` the whole-screen view, and both work offline once installed. It is not a rewrite — the app loads the extension's own pages, timer engine and sound synthesis unchanged, on a small adapter that maps `chrome.*` onto plain web APIs, so a fix lands in both at once. The stage-end badge becomes the window title and the taskbar dot; notifications and sounds work the same. Only one window ever owns the timer, so a second tab cannot double-fire a stage. The floating widget stays extension-only — it is a content script and cannot exist outside a browser.
- **Add a task from anywhere.** The fullscreen task card and the floating widget both grew a one-line input, so a thought can be captured without opening the side panel: type, press Enter, it appears in every surface at once. In fullscreen the ambient fade holds off while the field has focus, and in the widget the keystrokes are kept from reaching the host page's own keyboard shortcuts.
- **Deadlines everywhere too.** The same date chip the panel uses now sits on tasks in the fullscreen card and the floating widget, with the overdue (red) and due-soon (amber) highlighting that goes with it. The widget only rebuilds its list when a task actually changes, so the native date picker no longer closes under the twice-a-second redraw.
- **New brand identity** — "Firefly Focus" name and a firefly-in-a-timer-ring icon in the Moon palette, shipped as 16/32/48/128 px PNGs. The logo, wordmark and both store promo tiles share it.
- **Three themes** — Midnight (dark), Daylight (light) and Sage (calm green), each with its own tasteful accent palette. Theme switcher lives in the panel header, next to the language selector.
- **Task deadlines** — optional due date per task, with overdue (red) and due-soon (amber) highlighting in both the side panel and the floating widget.
- **Four more languages** — German, Spanish, Italian, Slovak and Czech (seven in total, alongside English and Ukrainian). Theme names stay untranslated.
- **Study buddy** — a pixel-art ginger cat with a touch of Maine Coon (lynx-tipped ears, a cream ruff, a big ringed tail) in a sage headband with two firefly antennae keeps you company in the side panel and the fullscreen timer. It faces you, with big green eyes that catch the light and a small "ω" smile. While the timer runs it walks the width of the screen on an eight-frame gait matched to its pace, so the paws never slide, and turns round at each end; stop the timer and it sits the way a real cat sits (traced from a photo); leave it stopped and it lies down, curls up and dozes with a "z" drifting up. It is never quite still: it breathes on a soft cubic-bezier curve, the whiskers sway, the tail swings after the body with a little lag, the antennae are springs that bob with every breath, step and turn, and it blinks at random. At night (Midnight, Sage) the bulbs glow steadily with a soft sage neon; in Daylight they save energy and only wink now and then. Drawn from shapes into canvases when the page opens and scaled to whole device pixels, so every pixel stays crisp and even: no image files, no requests. Turn it off with the cat button or "Cat study buddy" in the settings.
- **Day/night switch, a cat button and purring** — three small controls under the cat, and in the corner of the fullscreen timer. The sun/moon switch flips between Daylight and your night theme, and remembers whether that was Midnight or Sage. The cat button sends the cat off in a puff of sparkles and folds its lane away, or brings it back with a hop. The purr button — or a click on the cat itself — makes it purr: a purr synthesised live with Web Audio, the cat's chest rising and falling in time with it, a gentle vibration and happily closed eyes.
- **Three timer faces** — the countdown can be drawn as the **Ring** (unchanged), **Digits only** (no ring at all, just large numerals over a hairline of progress) or a **Breathing circle** (a soft glowing halo that slowly expands and contracts while the timer runs, and holds still when paused). Applies to the side panel and fullscreen; the floating widget always uses the ring.
- **Timer rhythm presets** — a picker above the duration fields fills them from a known technique: Classic 25/5, Deep work 50/10, DeskTime 52/17, Ultradian 90/20 or Short sprints 15/3. Editing any duration by hand drops it back to "Custom", so the label never disagrees with the numbers. Available in both the side panel and the fullscreen sheet.
- **Adjustable text size** — a "Text size" setting (80–140%) scales the interface text in the side panel, the fullscreen page and the floating widget. It applies the moment it changes: every click on the arrows saves it, so all three resize together without pressing Save; a typed value previews as you go and saves on Enter. The large timer digits stay fixed so they never overflow the ring.
- **Close button (×)** on the floating widget — a non-destructive dismiss that hides it on the current page only; it returns on reload, and the settings toggle remains the permanent on/off.
- **Fullscreen mode** — a "Fullscreen" button in the panel header opens the timer in its own tab as an ambient, whole-screen clock: a large ring, the current task, and today's progress. After four idle seconds the controls fade away and only the timer remains; any mouse move brings them back. It stays open and in sync when the side panel is closed.
- **Settings without leaving fullscreen** — a gear in the corner opens the full settings form, plus the task list, in a sheet over the timer. Durations, theme, language, sounds and tasks can all be changed without dropping out of the ambient view.
- **Draggable task card in fullscreen** — the open tasks sit in a small card you can drag anywhere on the screen; its position is remembered, and ticking one off works right there. A "Show tasks in fullscreen mode" setting hides it for a bare timer.
- **The floating widget comes in two pieces.** On websites the timer is a small card of its own — ring, time, play and pause — with your open tasks in a drawer underneath (the handle on its edge folds them away; "+ New task" adds one). Beside it, the study cat sits on a little shelf of buttons, the way a desktop companion sits over your work, and does one of two things, remembered across pages: it stays on the shelf and **plays with a ball** — turns to it, bats it with a paw, watches it roll off and bounce back, trots after it — or it jumps down and **strolls along the bottom of the tab**, sitting down here and there and now and then curling up for a nap. Each piece is dragged and remembered separately; a tap on the cat makes it purr. Tasks read like notifications: a round tick, the title, and the deadline as a second line when there is one. The cat is drawn in the page's first quiet moment, not while it loads.

### Changed
- **New palettes for Midnight and Daylight.** Midnight follows "Moon" (`#F5D5E0 · #6667AB · #7B337E · #420D4B · #210635`) — a deep violet night with a magenta focus accent and moonlit pink on the long break. Daylight follows "Twine" (`#F4E7FB · #F3DCDC · #F5BCBA · #E3AADD · #C8A8E9 · #C3C7F3`) — a lilac-to-blush page with rose, periwinkle and orchid accents. Both source palettes are pastel or near-black, so the mode accents are contrast-corrected tints and shades of those hues; every text and control pair clears WCAG AA. The firefly colours and the toolbar badge follow suit. **Sage is unchanged.**
- **Firefly animation reworked** — fireflies are now continuous and stay around the widget/panel instead of scattering across the screen; their colour follows the active theme and mode.
- **Sound signals redesigned** — warmer, more expressive melodic phrases with soft envelopes and a low-pass filter so they are pleasant, not harsh.
- **Refined visual design** — softer glass surfaces, smoother buttons, glowing progress ring, styled dropdowns and improved typography.
- **One typographic system across the panel and the floating widget** — matching weights and em-relative tracking, so the widget no longer reads as a different typeface. Both timers use tabular figures, so the digits stop shifting as the countdown runs.
- Theme names (**Midnight / Daylight / Sage**) and sound-style names (**Bright clean / Arcade / Bell / Soft**) now stay in English across every language, including Ukrainian.

### Removed
- The floating-widget **opacity** setting (replaced by a fixed, pleasant default that brightens on hover).

### Fixed
- **A stage end is announced once, and the stage after it is kept.** Between events Chrome puts the extension's background worker to sleep, and the end of a stage is what wakes it — together with the start-up check that catches up on ends missed while the browser was closed. Both finished the stage: two chimes and two notifications, or the break that had just begun was finished on the spot. Changes to the timer now happen one at a time, and a stage can only be finished once. The same race could leave the toolbar badge showing the state from before a click on Start or Pause.
- **The end comes on time even with under 30 seconds to go.** Chrome fires an extension's alarms at least 30 seconds apart, so resuming a stage with 20 seconds left, or a reminder set less than 30 seconds before the end, held the end back and the countdown sat on 0:00. It never showed while the extension was loaded unpacked, where that limit does not apply.
- **After a sleep or a closed browser the cycle picks up where it would have been.** If the computer slept or Chrome was closed through the end of a stage, the next stage now counts from when the last one ended, not from when Chrome noticed; if it would have finished by then too, it waits for Start instead of running hours late. A reminder that comes due after its stage is over is dropped.
- **The app keeps time in a hidden window.** A minimised or covered app window has its timers held back by Chrome, so a stage could end up to a minute late; the countdown to each alarm now runs in a worker.
- **Turning the extension off now takes the floating widget off open pages too.** Chrome leaves a content script running on pages that were already open when an extension is disabled, removed or reloaded, so the widget stayed on screen counting down a timer nothing could start or stop until the page was reloaded. It now notices within half a second and removes itself.
- **The fullscreen task card no longer opens on top of the corner buttons.** The first time it appears it sits under them.
- **The side panel no longer scrolls sideways.** The study buddy walks by sliding a track-wide element across the panel, and its box carried on past the right edge, giving the whole page a horizontal scroll range — at a narrow side-panel width the layout looked shifted and cut off. The track is now clipped on that axis only, so the drifting "z" still shows above it.
- **Section headings no longer push their button out of the card.** "Clear done" and "Reset stats" sit on a row with the heading; neither shrinks below its text, so on a narrow panel the button spilled past the card edge. The row now wraps and the button drops below, still right-aligned.
- **A paused timer no longer resets when a setting is saved.** Saving settings cleared the remaining time whenever the timer was not running, so changing the theme, language or a widget toggle mid-pause silently rewound the stage to full length. The countdown is now discarded only when the current stage's own duration actually changes.
- **Deadline date picker** no longer flickers or snaps shut — the task list is now only rebuilt when a task actually changes, not on every one-second timer tick.
- **Offscreen audio document** now closes itself shortly after each sound, so Chrome no longer shows the persistent background-document ("recording"-style) indicator on the extension while idle. No media is ever captured — the document only plays the timer sounds.
