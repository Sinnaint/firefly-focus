# Privacy Policy — Firefly Focus

_Last updated: 2026-11-06_

This policy covers Firefly Focus, the Windows program. It explains exactly what the program does and does not do with your information.

## Short version

**Firefly Focus does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own computer.** There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising, and we have no servers that could receive your data.

## What is stored, and where

Firefly Focus saves the following **on your computer only**, in one file in the program's own data folder:

- Timer state (current mode, whether it is running, remaining time).
- Your tasks, including any optional deadlines and completion status. Task text is whatever you type, so keep in mind that it may contain anything you choose to write there.
- Your settings (durations, theme, language, sound style, toggles and so on).
- Daily statistics (sessions completed today, focus time, totals).
- Where you last left the widgets and the on-screen icon, and what the cat is doing.

This data is used only to run the timer and show your tasks and statistics. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is protected the way your other files are, by your Windows account, and it is not encrypted separately.

## What Firefly Focus does NOT do

- ❌ No data is transmitted over the network, and no external requests are made. The program refuses network connections in its own code, so it stays local even if something in it were to try.
- ❌ No analytics, telemetry, crash reports, tracking pixels or advertising IDs.
- ❌ No automatic updater inside the program. Updates come through the Microsoft Store.
- ❌ No reading, collecting or transmitting of what you do in other programs.
- ❌ No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
- ❌ No cookies.

## "Uses all system resources"

Windows and the Microsoft Store list the program as being able to use "all system resources". This is how desktop programs work in general, and Firefly Focus needs it to draw its widgets and icon over your other programs. It does not use it to look at them: it does not read your screen, files, keystrokes or the contents of other windows. It sees the mouse pointer's position over the screen only to know when the pointer is on one of its own widgets, and it sees what you type only while you are typing into one of its own fields.

## The cat's game with desktop icons

This is optional, and off until you switch it on in the settings. While it is on, and only then, a small helper inside the program asks Windows for the positions of the icons on your desktop and for the position and order of the windows on the screen, to know which icons can be seen right now. When the ball hits an icon, it also fetches that icon's picture, to make a copy of it wobble.

It does not read the names of your icons or what they point to, and it never moves, opens or changes them. All of this stays in the program's memory while it runs. None of it is saved, and none of it is sent anywhere. Switching the game off ends the helper.

A bitten icon does not open with the mouse while the cat has hold of it, because the program's own transparent layer takes the click. The icon itself is unchanged, and it still opens by keyboard. Click the cat, drag it away or turn on the ball, and the layer lets go.

## Your control over your data

All of it is on your computer, so you can see, change and remove it at any time:

- Change or delete tasks and settings within Firefly Focus, and clear statistics with **Reset statistics** in the settings.
- Uninstalling the program removes its data as well.

## Children

Firefly Focus collects no information from anyone, children included.
